Support & FAQ

Answers to common questions about Muslim on iPhone and iPad.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are the prayer times, and which calculation method is used?

Muslim calculates prayer times with the Aladhan service using your coordinates, with the Muslim World League method and the Shafi school (for Asr). Times can differ by a few minutes from your local mosque, which may follow a different method or adjustment. If your mosque's times differ, treat the mosque as the authority.

Why does the app ask for my location, and what if I deny it?

Location (When In Use) is used only to compute prayer times for where you are and to point the Qibla compass. If you deny it, you can pick your city manually, but the Qibla compass needs location access. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Muslim.

Notifications or the Athan are not firing. What should I check?

1) In iOS Settings → Notifications → Muslim, make sure notifications and sounds are allowed. 2) Check that Focus modes, Silent mode, or Do Not Disturb are not muting alerts. 3) Open the app once after changing city or location so reminders are rescheduled. 4) Make sure the prayer reminders you want are switched on inside the app. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device, so no internet connection is required for them to fire.

The Qibla direction looks off. How do I calibrate?

Move away from magnets, metal objects, and electronics, hold the phone flat, and move it in a figure-8 motion to calibrate the compass. Also make sure Location Services and Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → System Services → Compass Calibration are on.

How do I change the app language?

Open the app's Settings → Language and choose your language. You can also set a per-app language in iOS Settings → Apps → Muslim → Language.

Does the app work offline?

Yes, partly. Qibla, tasbih, bookmarks, the tracker, and content already cached on your device work without internet. Loading new Quran content, prayer times for a new place, and word audio requires a connection.

Is my data private?

Yes. Muslim has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking SDKs. Your bookmarks, tracker, and settings stay on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Need help or found a bug?

If you need more help or run into a problem, open a request on our GitHub Issues page (please include your iOS version and steps to reproduce). You can also email devj666@gmail.com.
