Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

Summary

Muslim has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. We do not sell your personal information. Your bookmarks, favourites, tasbih counts, prayer tracker, and settings stay on your device. The only data that leaves your device are the content and prayer-time requests described below and feedback you choose to submit through the voluntary in-app feedback feature.

No Accounts, Ads, Analytics, or Tracking

  • The app has no account or sign-in system.
  • The app contains no advertising, no ad networks, no analytics, and no third-party tracking SDKs (for example, no Firebase).
  • The app’s privacy manifest declares that it does not track you. It declares OtherUserContent and EmailAddress solely for voluntary in-app feedback; this data is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking.
  • This website also uses no cookies, no analytics, and no trackers.

Location

If you allow it, Muslim uses your device's location (When In Use only) to calculate prayer times for where you are and to show the Qibla direction. We do not store your location on any server and we do not keep a history of it. To fetch prayer times, the app sends your coordinates (or your city and country) to the Aladhan prayer-times service (api.aladhan.com). To show your city name, the app uses Apple's MapKit reverse geocoding, which is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. You can deny or revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services; you can then choose your city manually.

Quran Text, Translations & Audio

Quran text and translations are loaded from api.alquran.cloud and api.quran.com, and word-by-word audio is streamed from verses.quran.com. These requests only ask for public content (for example, a chapter or a verse) and contain no personal data. As with any internet request, the operators of these services can technically see your IP address; they are not operated by us, and their handling of it is governed by their own policies. Some content may be cached on your device so it opens faster and works offline.

Data Stored on Your Device

Bookmarks, favourites, tasbih counts, the prayer tracker, and your settings (such as language and calculation preferences) are stored only on your device, using Apple's SwiftData and UserDefaults. We have no server that receives them. All of this data is permanently removed when you delete the app.

Notifications

Prayer-time and Athan reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Muslim has no push-notification server and does not send notifications from the internet. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications.

Voluntary In-App Feedback

Feedback is sent only when you actively enter it and tap Send. It includes the subject, your feedback, your optional email address, and the app version, iOS version, device model, and language. This information is stored in the developer’s Google Sheets and used to fix bugs and provide support. You can request its deletion at any time by emailing devj666@gmail.com.

Data Retention

Voluntary in-app feedback is retained in the developer’s Google Sheets only as long as needed to fix bugs and provide support, and can be deleted upon request via devj666@gmail.com. Data on your device is kept until you remove it in the app or delete the app.

Children's Privacy

The app contains no ads and no accounts, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy in the future, we will publish the updated version on this page and revise the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Visit our Support page or email devj666@gmail.com.
